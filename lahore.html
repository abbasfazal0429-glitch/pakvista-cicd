Lahore, City of Gardens

Lahore is one of Pakistan's most historic cities, celebrated for gardens, cuisine, literature, music, and grand Mughal monuments.

The Badshahi Mosque stands as a defining symbol of the city. Its red sandstone walls, marble domes, and vast courtyard reflect the ambition of Mughal architecture and remain a major gathering place for visitors.

Nearby Lahore Fort preserves royal halls, decorative tile work, and layered stories from several dynasties. The fort area connects naturally with the old city's gates, bazaars, and food streets.

Major Attractions

Badshahi Mosque

A monumental mosque built during the Mughal era, famous for its scale, symmetry, and evening views.

Lahore Fort

A UNESCO World Heritage site containing palaces, gates, museums, and decorated chambers from Lahore's royal past.

Shalimar Gardens

Terraced Mughal gardens with water channels, pavilions, and shaded paths that show Lahore's garden tradition.
