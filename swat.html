Swat Valley

Swat Valley is known for green hills, rivers, winter sports, local bazaars, Buddhist heritage, and mountain resorts.

Malam Jabba is a major attraction for skiing, chairlift rides, and high-altitude views. In summer it becomes a cool retreat for visitors escaping warmer cities.

Mingora Bazaar gives travelers a practical look at everyday Swat life, with local shops, food, handicrafts, and transport links to nearby valleys.

Visitor Stops

Malam Jabba

A mountain resort area with skiing in winter, scenic rides, and panoramic views across the valley.

Mingora Bazaar

A busy commercial area for local food, textiles, transport, and everyday shopping in Swat's main urban center.

Swat Museum

A museum preserving Gandhara-era artifacts, regional history, and cultural material from the Swat region.
