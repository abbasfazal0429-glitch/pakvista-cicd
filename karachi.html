Karachi, City of Lights

Karachi is Pakistan's largest city, a coastal center of trade, culture, food, museums, beaches, and fast-moving urban life.

Clifton Beach is one of the city's most visited seaside spots. Families, students, and tourists gather there for sea breeze, camel rides, street food, and sunset walks along the Arabian Sea.

Beyond the coast, Karachi offers heritage buildings, art spaces, shopping streets, and a food culture shaped by communities from across Pakistan.

Highlights

Clifton Beach

A public beach known for evening crowds, coastal views, local snacks, and a relaxed seaside atmosphere.

Mohatta Palace

A historic palace and museum space with distinctive stone architecture, exhibitions, and landscaped grounds.

Pakistan Maritime Museum

A museum with naval displays, maritime history, outdoor exhibits, and educational spaces for families and students.
