Hunza Valley

Hunza Valley in Gilgit-Baltistan is famous for high peaks, clear lakes, stone forts, terraced farms, and welcoming mountain communities.

The Rakaposhi viewpoint gives travelers a direct view of one of the region's most recognizable peaks. Roadside tea stops and viewing areas make it a popular pause on the Karakoram Highway.

Attabad Lake adds a striking turquoise landscape to the valley. Visitors can enjoy boating, photography, lakeside cafes, and mountain reflections formed after the natural lake appeared in 2010.

Places to See

Rakaposhi Viewpoint

A scenic stop for mountain views, local snacks, and memorable photographs of snow-covered peaks.

Attabad Lake

A bright blue lake surrounded by cliffs, tunnels, and boat routes that attract visitors throughout the travel season.

Baltit Fort

A restored historic fort above Karimabad that reflects Hunza's architecture, leadership history, and mountain setting.
